Revision B firmware verification | 23 November 2026

Teensy 4.1 release builds, PlatformIO Teensy 5.0.0, Teensyduino 1.59, ARM GCC 11.3.1.
LineBench and MainBench both compiled and linked. Their firmware.hex outputs are included next to the sketches, named for the corresponding sketch.

Host tests with simulated analog and digital inputs passed:
- Scanning all 32 signed ambient differences in address order
- Green/white calibration, exclusion of a low-contrast channel
- Separate white arcs, wrap-around at channels 31/0, interpolation with unequal neighboring signal strengths
- Five PWM outputs set to zero on stop; startup requires an observed STOP
- Active control-loop deadline, stale command, driver fault and sensor health cutoffs; restart with STOP then PLAY; millisecond-counter rollover
- OpenMV candidate and no-candidate frames consumed by the Teensy decoder, CRC rejection, recovery from a shifted stream and a duplicate sequence failing to refresh data age

The OpenMV script ran in a host harness with camera and UART simulators. Motor torque, stopping distance, LED settling, optical classification accuracy, physical emergency-stop timing, thermal behavior and match performance still require bench and field measurements.
